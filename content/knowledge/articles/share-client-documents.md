# Sharing Documents Securely with Clients

Email attachments create scattered threads, version confusion and security concerns. The Portal Genie gives you one secure place to share documents with clients.

## Accounting documents from your integration

When your accounting software is connected, invoices, statements, quotes and credit notes from **Xero, QuickBooks Online or Sage Business Cloud** appear in your Client Portal automatically.

Clients access these documents through secure portal login — not via emailed PDFs.

Learn about your integration options in [Connecting Portal Genie to Your Accounting Software](/resources/accounting-integrations).

## Uploaded and custom documents

Your team can upload additional documents for clients — contracts, reports, letters or any file your clients need to access. Assign documents to the relevant client and place them in the appropriate folder.

See [Organising Client Documents with Folders](/resources/client-portal-folders) for folder structure guidance.

## Client assignment and visibility

Documents are assigned to specific clients so each person sees only what is relevant to them. Folder visibility settings control what appears in each client's portal view.

## Secure access and downloads

Clients sign in through the secure OTP process described in [How Clients Access Your Portal](/resources/client-portal-access). Once verified, they can view and download documents from their assigned folders.

## Notifying clients about new documents

Enable **new document notifications** so clients receive an email when documents are assigned and available in their portal.

Read [Notify Clients When New Documents Are Available](/resources/new-document-notifications).

## Client uploads (Pro)

On **Pro**, clients can upload documents to folders you permit — useful for receiving signed forms, tax records or supporting files.

See [Client Document Uploads](/resources/client-document-uploads).
