# What Can Clients Do in the Client Portal?

Your Client Portal gives clients a secure, branded destination for everyday account tasks — reducing phone calls, emails and resend requests.

Here is what clients can typically access and do.

## Accounting documents

When your accounting software is connected, clients can view and download:

- Invoices
- Statements
- Quotes
- Credit notes

Documents appear in organised folders so clients can find what they need quickly. See [Organising Client Documents with Folders](/resources/client-portal-folders).

## Custom and uploaded documents

Your team can upload additional documents for clients to access — such as contracts, reports or supporting files. On **Pro**, clients can also upload documents to folders you permit.

Learn more in [Sharing Documents Securely with Clients](/resources/share-client-documents) and [Client Document Uploads](/resources/client-document-uploads).

## Pay Now — online invoice payments

Clients can select invoices and pay through **Pay Now** when you have connected a supported payment provider such as **Stripe** or **Peach Payments**.

See [Let Clients Pay Invoices Through the Client Portal](/resources/client-portal-payments).

## Notice Board

Your **Notice Board** is a display space within the portal for announcements, promotions, seasonal reminders and important messages.

Read more in [Using the Notice Board](/resources/notice-board).

## Document notes (Pro)

On **Pro**, clients and your team can exchange **document notes** — messages attached to specific documents that keep conversations in context.

See [Document Notes and Client Communication](/resources/document-notes).

## Document statuses and tags

You can label documents with custom statuses so clients understand where things stand — for example, "Awaiting approval" or "Action required".

## Desktop and mobile experience

The Client Portal is designed to work on desktop and mobile devices. Clients can access documents, make payments and read your Notice Board from the device that suits them.

## Customisation

Your portal reflects your business branding — logo, colours, welcome messaging and folder structure. Pro adds further options such as your own portal domain and removal of Portal Genie branding.

Explore [Customising Your Client Portal](/resources/customise-client-portal) and [Brand the Client Portal as Your Own](/resources/client-portal-branding).

Compare plan features in [Portal Genie Core vs Pro](/resources/core-vs-pro) or [Book a demo](/book-a-demo).
