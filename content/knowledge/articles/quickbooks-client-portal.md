# Portal Genie for QuickBooks Online

Connect **QuickBooks Online** to The Portal Genie and deliver a branded Client Portal where clients access accounting documents and manage their relationship with your business.

## What connects from QuickBooks Online

When you connect QuickBooks Online, Portal Genie brings client records and accounting documents into your Client Portal, including:

- **Customer contacts** — enabling secure portal access
- **Invoices** — for client viewing and online payment
- **Statements** — account summaries in organised folders
- **Quotes** — shared with clients as needed
- **Credit notes** — visible alongside related documents

## Client access through QuickBooks contacts

Clients log in using the email address on their QuickBooks Online customer record. Portal Genie sends a secure OTP to verify their identity — no separate Portal Genie registration required.

See [How Clients Access Your Portal](/resources/client-portal-access).

## Pay Now for QuickBooks invoices

When a payment provider such as **Stripe** or **Peach Payments** is connected, clients can pay QuickBooks Online invoices through **Pay Now** in their portal.

Read [Let Clients Pay Invoices Through the Client Portal](/resources/client-portal-payments).

## Works alongside QuickBooks Online

The Portal Genie does not replace QuickBooks Online. Your team continues managing accounts in QuickBooks while clients use the portal for self-service access.

## Getting started

1. Connect QuickBooks Online in your Portal Genie account
2. Customise your portal branding and folders
3. Share your **Client Portal Link** with clients
