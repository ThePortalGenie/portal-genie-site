# Using Your Own Client Portal Domain

A custom portal domain gives clients a web address they recognise — such as **portal.yourcompany.com** — rather than a generic platform URL.

## A Pro capability

Using your own portal web address is available on the **Pro** plan. Core provides full portal functionality with Portal Genie hosting; Pro lets you present the portal on your own domain.

Compare plans in [Portal Genie Core vs Pro](/resources/core-vs-pro).

## Why businesses use a custom domain

A custom portal domain:

- Reinforces your brand at every client touchpoint
- Builds trust — clients see a URL that belongs to your business
- Creates a professional, cohesive experience alongside your main website
- Makes your Client Login button feel like a natural part of your online presence

## How setup works

Setting up a custom domain involves configuring DNS records to point your chosen subdomain to Portal Genie. Your Portal Genie account guides you through the verification process.

Typical examples:

- `portal.yourcompany.com`
- `clients.yourcompany.com`
- `my.yourcompany.com`

DNS values are specific to your setup — follow the instructions in your Portal Genie account rather than using generic examples from external sources.

## Works with your website Client Login

Your custom domain replaces the default Client Portal Link. Update your website **Client Login** button to point to your new address so clients always arrive at your branded portal.

See [Add a Client Login Button to Your Existing Website](/resources/add-client-login-to-your-website).
