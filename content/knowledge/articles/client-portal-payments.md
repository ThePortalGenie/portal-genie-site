# Let Clients Pay Invoices Through the Client Portal

Make it simple for clients to view and pay invoices through **Pay Now** in your Client Portal — reducing payment delays and follow-up calls.

## How Pay Now works

The typical payment flow:

1. **Client opens their portal** and navigates to invoices
2. **Client selects one or more invoices** to pay
3. **Client clicks Pay Now** and completes payment through your connected provider
4. **Payment result is recorded** — confirming the transaction outcome

Clients pay from within the portal experience they already use for documents and communication.

## Supported payment providers

Pay Now works when you connect a supported payment provider. Confirmed providers include:

- **Stripe**
- **Peach Payments**

Payment integration must be configured in your Portal Genie account before clients can pay through the portal.

## Invoices from your accounting software

Invoices surfaced from **Xero, QuickBooks Online or Sage Business Cloud** appear in your Client Portal and can be selected for payment when Pay Now is enabled.

See your platform guide:

- [Portal Genie for Xero](/resources/xero-client-portal)
- [Portal Genie for QuickBooks Online](/resources/quickbooks-client-portal)

## Client invoice payments vs your subscription

Pay Now handles **client invoice payments** — money your clients pay to your business through the portal.

This is separate from your **Portal Genie subscription billing** — the monthly fee your business pays for the platform.
