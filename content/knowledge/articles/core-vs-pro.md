# Portal Genie Core vs Pro

The Portal Genie offers two plans — **Core** and **Pro** — so you can choose the client portal features that match your business today and upgrade as you grow.

Both plans include a **30-day free trial**.

## Core — the complete client portal experience

Core delivers the full foundation of client portal software:

- Customise your client portal with logo and brand colours
- Automatic integration of invoices, statements, quotes and credit notes from your accounting software
- **Pay Now** — clients pay invoices through your portal
- Upload documents for clients to access
- Display space on your **Notice Board**
- Internal notes and reminders for your team
- Custom document statuses and tags
- Custom folders for document organisation

Core includes **2 users** and **2 GB** of storage.

Core portals display a small "Brought to you by The Portal Genie" logo.

## Pro — everything in Core, plus

Pro adds advanced communication, branding and security capabilities:

- **Remove The Portal Genie branding**
- **Use your own portal web address** (e.g. portal.yourcompany.com)
- **Send emails from your own domain**
- **Let clients upload documents** for you to receive
- **Document notes** — clients create and respond to notes on documents
- **Schedule email campaigns** with client groups
- **Email documents directly into the portal**
- **Extra password protection** for your portal

Pro includes **2 users**, **10 GB** of storage and **3,000 emails per month** for scheduled campaigns.

## Add-ons

Both plans support add-ons as your business grows:

- Additional storage (per GB or 1 TB bundles)
- Additional email credits (Pro)
- Additional users (per user/month)

## Which plan is right for you?

Choose **Core** if you need a branded client portal with accounting integration, document sharing, Pay Now and the Notice Board.

Choose **Pro** if you also need client uploads, document notes, scheduled email campaigns, your own domain or removal of Portal Genie branding.
