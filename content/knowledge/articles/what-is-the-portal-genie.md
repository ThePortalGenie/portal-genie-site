# What is The Portal Genie?

The Portal Genie is **client portal software** that helps businesses deliver a modern, connected customer experience.

It brings together **documents, communication, payments and self-service** in one **branded Client Portal** — working alongside the accounting software you already use.

## A customer experience layer, not accounting software

Great accounting software manages finances. Client experience often sits outside those core workflows.

The Portal Genie exists as a **customer experience layer** for businesses. Your team continues working with familiar accounting tools while clients gain a secure, branded portal.

The Portal Genie **does not replace** your accounting platform. It extends the client experience while your team keeps using the systems they already know.

## Who is The Portal Genie for?

The Portal Genie is built for businesses that want to:

- Give clients one secure place to access documents and account information
- Reduce repetitive admin through client self-service
- Present a professional, branded client experience
- Connect their accounting software to a modern Client Portal

Accountants, bookkeepers, finance teams and business owners use The Portal Genie to improve how clients interact with their business.

## What The Portal Genie brings together

Through one connected platform, you can offer clients:

- Secure access to invoices, statements, quotes and credit notes from your accounting software
- Document sharing and organised folder structure
- Online payments through **Pay Now**
- A **Notice Board** for announcements and promotions
- Client communication through document notes (Pro)
- Client document uploads (Pro)
- Branding and customisation to match your business

For a full overview of capabilities, see the [Features](/features) page.
