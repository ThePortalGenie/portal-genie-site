# How The Portal Genie Client Portal Works

The Portal Genie connects your business, your accounting software and your clients through a single branded Client Portal.

Your team continues working in your accounting system. Clients access their documents, payments and communication through a secure portal designed for self-service.

## The relationship at a glance

1. **Your business** subscribes to The Portal Genie and connects your accounting software
2. **Your accounting software** supplies client contacts and accounting documents
3. **The Portal Genie** presents those documents in a branded Client Portal
4. **Your clients** access the portal through your Client Portal Link — no separate registration required

## What your team does

Your team manages the portal from your Portal Genie account:

- Connect and maintain your accounting integration
- Customise branding, folders and the Notice Board
- Share and upload documents for clients
- Communicate through document notes (Pro)
- Monitor client engagement and portal activity

Day-to-day accounting work stays in your accounting software. The Portal Genie handles the client-facing experience.

## What your clients see

Clients open your **Client Portal Link** and sign in with a secure email OTP. Once verified, they can:

- View invoices, statements, quotes and credit notes
- Download documents from organised folders
- Pay invoices through **Pay Now** (when payment integration is connected)
- Read announcements on your **Notice Board**
- Upload documents to permitted folders (Pro)
- Exchange document notes with your team (Pro)

See [What Can Clients Do in the Client Portal?](/resources/client-portal-features) for a full overview.

## How clients get in

Clients do not register for a Portal Genie account. If they exist as a contact in your connected accounting software with the correct email address, they can access the portal through the secure OTP process.

Read the step-by-step guide: [How Clients Access Your Portal](/resources/client-portal-access).

Every account includes a **Client Portal Link** you can add to your website as a Client Login button or share directly with clients.
