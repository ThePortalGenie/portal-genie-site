# Portal Genie for Xero

The Portal Genie is an official **Xero Connected App** that brings your Xero client data and accounting documents into a secure, branded Client Portal.

Your team keeps working in Xero. Clients get self-service access to documents, payments and communication.

## What connects from Xero

When you connect Xero, Portal Genie typically surfaces:

- **Contacts** — client records that enable secure portal access
- **Invoices** — available for clients to view and pay
- **Statements** — account summaries in the client portal
- **Quotes** — shared with clients for review
- **Credit notes** — visible alongside other accounting documents

Documents appear in organised folders within your Client Portal.

## Client access through Xero contacts

Clients access the portal using the email address stored against their Xero contact record. The secure OTP login process matches their email to their contact — no separate registration required.

Read [How Clients Access Your Portal](/resources/client-portal-access).

## Pay Now with Xero invoices

Clients can select Xero invoices and pay through **Pay Now** when you connect a supported payment provider such as **Stripe** or **Peach Payments**.

See [Let Clients Pay Invoices Through the Client Portal](/resources/client-portal-payments).

## Additional contacts

Where applicable, additional contacts linked to a Xero client record may also access the portal, depending on your configuration and contact setup.

## Does Portal Genie replace Xero?

No. Portal Genie is the **customer experience layer** for businesses using Xero. Your team continues managing accounts in Xero while clients interact through the portal.
