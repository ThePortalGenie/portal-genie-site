# How Clients Access Your Portal — No Registration Required

One of the most practical benefits of The Portal Genie is that **your clients do not need to register for Portal Genie**.

If a client exists in your connected accounting software with the appropriate email address, they can access your Client Portal through a secure one-time passcode (OTP) sent to their email.

No additional client registration. No separate Portal Genie password to remember.

## How the access flow works

When a client wants to open your portal, the journey looks like this:

1. **Client opens your Client Portal Link** — from your website, an email or a direct link you have shared
2. **Client enters their email address** — the same email address stored against their contact in your accounting software
3. **Portal Genie identifies the client** — matching the email to the corresponding client or contact record
4. **A secure OTP is sent** — a one-time passcode arrives in the client's email inbox
5. **Client enters the OTP** — verifying their identity
6. **The Client Portal opens** — the client can view their documents, make payments and use portal features

This is not the client creating a Portal Genie account. It is secure, email-verified access tied to their existing relationship with your business.

## What clients need

For a client to access your portal, they need:

- A contact record in your connected accounting software (Xero, QuickBooks Online or Sage Business Cloud)
- The correct email address on that contact record
- Access to that email inbox to receive the OTP

If a client's email has changed, update it in your accounting software so the OTP reaches the right inbox.

## Sharing the Client Portal Link

You can give clients access in several ways:

- Add your **Client Portal Link** to a **Client Login** button on your website
- Include the link in emails and client communications
- Share the link directly with individual clients

See [Add a Client Login Button to Your Existing Website](/resources/add-client-login-to-your-website) for more on the website login option.

## Security without friction

The OTP process balances security with simplicity. Clients verify their identity through email without managing another password.
