# What can customers access through The Portal Genie?

The Portal Genie gives customers a **branded online portal** — a secure destination where they can interact with your business on their terms.

## Customer Portal

Customers can serve themselves through a branded portal that provides **secure self-service access**. Every Portal Genie account includes a unique portal link you can share with customers or connect to a Client Login button on your website.

## Document Management

Customers can access and retrieve documents when they need them. Businesses can share documents through the portal and receive documents uploaded by customers — helping keep document history organised in one place rather than scattered across email attachments.

## Online Payments

Customers can make **secure online payments** through the portal, including paying multiple invoices where payment options are available in your setup.

## Customer Communication

The Portal Genie provides a dedicated communication layer so conversations between customers and your team can stay **organised in one place**, with communication history tied to customer records.

## Branding and professional presentation

Businesses can present a **consistent, professional experience** that reflects their brand across the customer portal.

## Administration and security

The platform is built as a **secure cloud platform for business use**, with administrative controls to manage users and platform settings.
