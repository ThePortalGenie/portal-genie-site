# Portal Genie for Sage Business Cloud

Connect **Sage Business Cloud** to The Portal Genie and give clients a secure, branded portal for accounting documents and self-service access.

## What connects from Sage Business Cloud

When you connect Sage Business Cloud, Portal Genie surfaces client records and accounting documents in your Client Portal, including:

- **Customer contacts** — the basis for secure portal login
- **Invoices** — available for clients to view and pay
- **Statements** — organised in client-facing folders
- **Quotes and credit notes** — shared alongside other accounting documents

## Client access through Sage contacts

Clients access the portal using the email address on their Sage Business Cloud contact record. A secure OTP verifies their identity — with no separate Portal Genie account to create.

Read [How Clients Access Your Portal](/resources/client-portal-access).

## Pay Now for Sage invoices

Connect a supported payment provider such as **Stripe** or **Peach Payments** to enable **Pay Now**, letting clients pay Sage invoices directly through their portal.

See [Let Clients Pay Invoices Through the Client Portal](/resources/client-portal-payments).

## Works alongside Sage Business Cloud

The Portal Genie extends the client experience without replacing Sage. Your team keeps working in Sage Business Cloud while clients interact through the portal.

## Getting started

1. Connect Sage Business Cloud in your Portal Genie account
2. Configure branding, folders and your Notice Board
3. Share your **Client Portal Link** — on your website or directly with clients

For the broader integration picture, see [Connecting Portal Genie to Your Accounting Software](/resources/accounting-integrations).
