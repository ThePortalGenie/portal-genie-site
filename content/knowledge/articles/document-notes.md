# Document Notes and Client Communication

Scattered email threads make it hard to know which message relates to which document. **Document notes** keep client communication tied to the documents they relate to.

## What document notes are

Document notes are messages attached to a specific document in your Client Portal. Both your team and the client can read and respond, creating a clear conversation history in context.

This is distinct from **internal notes** — staff-only reminders visible only to your team within your Portal Genie account.

## How they work

1. A document is shared with a client in their portal
2. Either party adds a note on that document
3. The other party can read and reply
4. The conversation history stays attached to the document

Notes help clients ask questions about a specific invoice, query a statement line or clarify an uploaded file — without starting a separate email thread.

## Read and unread status

Document notes support read and unread indicators so your team can see which conversations need attention and clients know when you have responded.

## Available on Pro

Document notes — including client-created notes and replies — are a **Pro** feature. Core includes internal staff notes; Pro extends communication to the client-facing portal.
