# Brand the Client Portal as Your Own

First impressions matter. Your Client Portal should look and feel like your business — with your logo, colours and messaging front and centre.

## Core branding features

On **Core**, you can customise:

- **Your logo** — displayed prominently in the portal
- **Brand colours** — applied across the client experience
- **Welcome messaging** — greet clients in your voice
- **Notice Board content** — your announcements and promotions
- **Folder and document presentation** — structured around your clients' needs

Core includes a small "Brought to you by The Portal Genie" logo on the portal.

## Pro branding enhancements

**Pro** adds further branding capabilities:

- **Remove The Portal Genie branding** — a cleaner, fully yours presentation
- **Your own portal web address** — such as portal.yourcompany.com
- **Send emails from your own domain** — client communications from your business address

See [Using Your Own Client Portal Domain](/resources/custom-client-portal-domain).

## Why branding matters

A branded portal:

- Builds client trust and confidence
- Reinforces your professional identity
- Makes the portal feel like part of your business, not a third-party tool
- Supports consistent communication across documents, payments and messaging

## Customisation beyond branding

Branding works alongside structural customisation — folders, document statuses and the Notice Board all contribute to the overall client experience.

Explore [Customising Your Client Portal](/resources/customise-client-portal).
