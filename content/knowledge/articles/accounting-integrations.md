# Connecting Portal Genie to Your Accounting Software

The Portal Genie works alongside your accounting software — bringing client contacts and accounting documents into a branded Client Portal without replacing how your team manages accounts.

## Supported accounting platforms

Portal Genie integrates with:

- **Xero**
- **QuickBooks Online**
- **Sage Business Cloud**

Each integration connects your client records and accounting documents to your Client Portal.

## What the integration delivers

When connected, your accounting software supplies:

- **Client and contact records** — the foundation for secure portal access
- **Invoices, statements, quotes and credit notes** — surfaced in organised portal folders
- **Contact email addresses** — used for secure OTP client login

Your team continues working in your accounting software. The Portal Genie presents the client-facing layer.

## Why connect your accounting software?

Integration means:

- Clients see up-to-date accounting documents in their portal
- You avoid manually uploading every invoice or statement
- Client access is tied to contact records you already maintain
- Your portal stays aligned with your accounting data
