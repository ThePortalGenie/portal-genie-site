# What is The Portal Genie?

The Portal Genie is **customer portal software** that helps businesses deliver a modern, connected customer experience.

It brings together **documents, communication, payments and self-service** in one **branded experience** — working alongside the accounting software you already use.

## A customer experience layer, not accounting software

Great accounting software manages finances. Customer experience often sits outside those core workflows.

The Portal Genie exists as a **customer experience layer** for businesses. Your team can continue working with familiar accounting tools and workflows while customers gain a secure, branded portal.

The Portal Genie **does not replace** your accounting platform. It extends the customer experience while your team keeps using the systems they already know.

## Works alongside your accounting software

The Portal Genie works with **Xero, QuickBooks and Sage Business Cloud**, integrating with the accounting software you already use rather than replacing it.

This means you can modernise how customers interact with your business without rethinking how your team manages accounts.

## What The Portal Genie brings together

Through one connected platform, businesses can offer customers:

- Secure access to documents
- Organised customer communication
- Online payments
- Customer self-service
