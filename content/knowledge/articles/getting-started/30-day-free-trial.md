# How does the 30-day free trial work?

The Portal Genie offers a **30-day free trial** on both **Core** and **Pro** plans so you can explore the platform before subscribing.

## Which plans include a trial?

**Core** and **Pro** both include a 30-day free trial.

Compare what is included in each plan on the [Pricing](/pricing) page.

## Getting started

You can start a 30-day free trial on Core or Pro and begin setting up your customer portal from the plan options on the [Pricing](/pricing#plans) page.

For current plan pricing in your currency, always refer to the live [Pricing](/pricing) page — prices are not listed here.

## Changing plans later

You can move between Core and Pro as your business needs change. Use the plan comparison on the Pricing page to see what is included at each tier.
