# How Portal Genie Keeps Client Portal Access Secure

Client portals handle sensitive documents and financial information. The Portal Genie is built as a secure cloud platform for business use, with access controls designed for both your team and your clients.

## Secure client access with email OTP

Clients do not use a standing Portal Genie password. Instead, they verify their identity through a **secure one-time passcode (OTP)** sent to their email address.

This process:

- Matches the client's email to their accounting software contact record
- Sends a time-limited OTP to that email inbox
- Grants portal access only after successful verification

Read the full flow in [How Clients Access Your Portal](/resources/client-portal-access).

## Optional portal password (Pro)

On **Pro**, you can add **extra password protection** to your portal — an additional layer beyond the OTP process for businesses that want it.

## Team user permissions

Your team accesses Portal Genie through user accounts with **role-based permissions**. Control what each team member can view, manage and configure.

See [Managing Team Users and Permissions](/resources/users-and-permissions).

## Multi-factor authentication for your team

Business users can enable **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** on their Portal Genie accounts for additional account security.

## Client and contact relationship

Client access is tied to contact records in your connected accounting software. Only clients with a matching email address on their contact record can receive an OTP and access the portal.

## Secure cloud platform

The Portal Genie is designed as a secure cloud platform for business use — handling documents, communication and payments with appropriate access controls.

## Custom domains and security

Using your own portal domain (Pro) does not change the underlying security model — clients still verify through the OTP process.

See [Using Your Own Client Portal Domain](/resources/custom-client-portal-domain).
