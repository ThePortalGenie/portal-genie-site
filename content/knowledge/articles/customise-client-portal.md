# Customising Your Client Portal

Your Client Portal should feel like an extension of your business — not a generic third-party tool.

The Portal Genie gives you practical customisation options so clients see your brand, find documents easily and stay informed through your Notice Board.

## Branding and presentation

Apply your business identity across the portal:

- **Logo** — display your company logo prominently
- **Brand colours** — match your existing brand palette
- **Welcome messaging** — greet clients with text that reflects your tone and service

On **Pro**, you can also remove The Portal Genie branding and use your own portal web address. See [Brand the Client Portal as Your Own](/resources/client-portal-branding).

## Folders and document organisation

Structure documents with standard accounting folders and custom folders you create. Control which folders are visible, their display order and which accept client uploads (Pro).

Read [Organising Client Documents with Folders](/resources/client-portal-folders).

## Document statuses and tags

Label documents with your own statuses so clients understand what action — if any — is needed. This reduces follow-up emails and keeps everyone aligned.

## Notice Board

Use the **Notice Board** as your portal display space for announcements, promotions and seasonal reminders. Switch content as your business needs change.

See [Using the Notice Board](/resources/notice-board).

## Preview before you publish

Review how your portal looks on desktop and mobile before clients see it. Check that branding, folders and the Notice Board present the experience you intend.

## Core vs Pro customisation

**Core** includes logo, brand colours, folder customisation, document statuses and the Notice Board — with a small "Brought to you by The Portal Genie" logo on the portal.

**Pro** adds your own portal domain, email domain, removal of Portal Genie branding and additional communication features.

Compare plans in [Portal Genie Core vs Pro](/resources/core-vs-pro) or view [Pricing](/pricing).
