# HOMEPAGE

## Purpose

The homepage is the primary entry point to The Portal Genie website.

Its purpose is to clearly communicate what The Portal Genie is, who it is for, and why it exists. It should guide visitors from curiosity to confidence before encouraging them to book a demonstration.

The homepage should tell a story rather than simply present features.

---

# Target Audience

The homepage is designed primarily for:

- Small to medium businesses using Xero
- Business owners
- Directors
- Office managers
- Finance managers
- Professional service businesses
- Growing companies seeking a better customer experience

Secondary audiences include:

- Accountants
- Bookkeepers
- Implementation partners
- Referral partners

---

# Primary Goal

The primary goal of the homepage is to generate demo bookings.

Every section should build confidence and reduce friction towards the primary call to action.

---

# Homepage Structure

The homepage will consist of the following sections in order.

1. Hero
2. Trust Bar
3. The Problem
4. Why The Portal Genie
5. Platform Overview
6. Core Features
7. Customer Outcomes
8. Frequently Asked Questions
9. Final Call to Action

Each section has a specific purpose and should naturally lead visitors to the next.

---

# Section 1 — Hero

## Goal

Immediately explain what The Portal Genie is.

Visitors should understand within a few seconds:

- what the platform does
- who it is for
- how it relates to Xero
- what they should do next

## Eyebrow

Built for Xero businesses

## Headline

The Customer Experience Layer for Xero businesses.

## Supporting Copy

Transform how your customers interact with your business without changing the way your team works in Xero.

Deliver a modern, self-service experience that strengthens customer relationships, reduces administration, and keeps everyone connected.

## Primary CTA

Book a Demo

## Secondary CTA

See the Platform

## Hero Visual

A premium browser mock-up displaying the Portal Genie application.

The visual should demonstrate a real product rather than an illustration.

---

# Section 2 — Trust Bar

## Goal

Build immediate credibility.

Possible items include:

- Built for Xero businesses
- Secure cloud platform
- Customer portal
- Document management
- Payments
- Automation

This section should be subtle and not dominate the page.

---

# Section 3 — The Problem

## Goal

Help visitors recognise their current frustrations.

Examples include:

- Endless email chains
- Customers requesting the same documents
- Difficulty tracking communications
- Fragmented customer experience
- Time wasted on administration

Visitors should recognise their own business before being introduced to the solution.

---

# Section 4 — Why The Portal Genie

## Goal

Introduce the platform as the missing customer experience layer.

Explain that businesses already have accounting software.

The Portal Genie improves the customer experience that sits around it.

Avoid positioning the platform as accounting software.

---

# Section 5 — Platform Overview

## Goal

Provide a high-level overview of the platform.

Include:

- screenshots
- workflow
- customer portal
- document management
- communication
- integrations

Focus on clarity rather than technical depth.

---

# Section 6 — Core Features

Present the major platform capabilities.

Examples:

- Customer Portal
- Secure Documents
- Digital Forms
- Messaging
- Payments
- Automation
- Xero Integration

Each feature should explain the customer benefit rather than simply describe functionality.

---

# Section 7 — Customer Outcomes

Focus on business outcomes rather than software features.

Examples:

- Save time
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Reduce administration
- Deliver a premium customer experience
- Increase efficiency
- Centralise customer communication

---

# Section 8 — Frequently Asked Questions

Address common questions before visitors ask them.

Examples include:

- Does it replace Xero?
- Is it difficult to implement?
- Can customers access documents securely?
- Can it be customised?
- Is support included?

---

# Section 9 — Final Call to Action

Finish with a strong invitation to book a demonstration.

Reinforce the value proposition.

Primary CTA:

Book a Demo

---

# Tone of Voice

The homepage should feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Confident
- Helpful
- Premium

Avoid:

- Marketing hype
- Buzzwords
- Technical jargon
- Unsubstantiated claims

The copy should be concise, clear, and customer-focused.

---

# Design Principles

The homepage should follow the design system.

Prioritise:

- generous whitespace
- clear visual hierarchy
- restrained colour usage
- premium typography
- responsive layouts
- accessibility

Every section should have a clear purpose and naturally guide visitors toward booking a demo.
