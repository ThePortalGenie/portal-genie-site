# Vision

Create the definitive website for Portal Genie.


## The Customer Experience Layer for Xero Businesses.

The website should position Portal Genie as an essential extension to Xero that transforms the customer experience without changing the accounting system businesses already know and trust.

Visitors should immediately understand:

> **"I already use Xero. Portal Genie is the missing piece."**

The website should feel like a premium SaaS platform that belongs naturally within the Xero ecosystem.

---

# Core Positioning

## Xero Runs Your Business.

## Portal Genie Runs Your Customer Experience.

Xero is one of the world's leading accounting platforms.

It manages finances, invoices, accounting and business processes.

Portal Genie does something different.

Portal Genie manages the experience that customers have with businesses using Xero.

Portal Genie sits between a Xero business and the customers it serves.

It transforms accounting information into an engaging, secure and branded customer experience.

Portal Genie never replaces Xero.

Portal Genie enhances Xero.

# The Problem

Xero is excellent at running a business.

It is not designed to deliver a modern customer experience.

Businesses still spend significant time:

- Sending invoices repeatedly
- Sending statements repeatedly
- Sending quotations
- Sharing documents
- Chasing payments
- Responding to customer requests
- Managing customer communications
- Maintaining document security
- Providing account information

Much of this still happens through email.

The experience is fragmented.

Time consuming.

Difficult to scale.

---

# The Solution

Portal Genie sits between Xero and the customer.

Portal Genie transforms accounting information into a secure branded customer experience.

Customers receive a modern self-service portal where they can:

- View invoices
- View statements
- View quotations
- Download documents
- Upload documents
- Complete payments
- Access account information
- Find everything they need 24/7

Businesses continue working inside Xero exactly as they do today.

Portal Genie simply transforms how customers experience that business.
