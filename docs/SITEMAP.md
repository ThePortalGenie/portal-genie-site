## 4. Homepage structure

Recommended section order:

| # | Section | Purpose |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Header | Global navigation |
| 2 | Hero | Introduce Portal Genie as the Customer Experience Layer |
| 3 | Trust or credibility | Reinforce fit for Xero businesses (without implying official Xero endorsement) |
| 4 | Problem | Xero excels at running a business; customer experience still happens through fragmented email and manual effort |
| 5 | Positioning | The Customer Experience Layer — Xero → Portal Genie → Customers |
| 6 | Platform overview | Real screenshots, real workflows, product visible early |
| 7 | Feature highlights | Outcome-led capabilities linked to customer experience |
| 8 | Xero relationship | Portal Genie works with Xero, enhances Xero, never replaces it |
| 9 | Audience or use cases | Speak to owners, finance teams, operations, and practice staff |
| 10 | Customer proof | Testimonials, case studies, business outcomes (when available) |
| 11 | FAQ | Reduce uncertainty; answer implementation questions |
| 12 | Final CTA | Book a Demo |
| 13 | Footer | Site links, legal, login, demo |

**Homepage goal (within five seconds)**  
Visitors should understand: I use Xero; Portal Genie works with Xero; it improves my customer's experience; it reduces administration; it is simple to implement; I should book a demo.

## 6. User journeys

### SME owner / managing director

**Goal**  
Understand quickly whether Portal Genie solves a real customer experience problem without disrupting Xero.

**Typical path**

1. Home → Hero and problem sections
2. Why The Portal Genie → category and outcomes
3. The Platform → product proof
4. Book a Demo

**Key pages**  
Home, Why The Portal Genie, The Platform, Pricing

---

### Accountant or bookkeeper

**Goal**  
Evaluate whether Portal Genie helps clients serve their customers better with less admin, and whether it fits alongside Xero.

**Typical path**

1. Home or Resources → educational content
2. Features → outcome-led capabilities
3. The Platform → workflows and customer-facing experience
4. Book a Demo or Contact

**Key pages**  
Resources, Features, The Platform, Contact

---

### Finance or office manager

**Goal**  
Assess day-to-day impact: less repeated document sending, fewer support requests, faster payments, secure self-service.

**Typical path**

1. Home → problem and platform overview
2. Features → specific outcomes (invoices, statements, documents, payments)
3. The Platform → screenshots and workflows
4. Pricing → value conversation
5. Book a Demo

**Key pages**  
Home, Features, The Platform, Pricing

---

### Existing customer (login or support)

**Goal**  
Access the Portal Genie product or find support — not evaluate the marketing site.

**Typical path**

1. Header or Footer → Login
2. If support needed → Contact (with clear routing to product support, not sales)

**Key pages**  
Login (external), Contact (support routing only)

**Note**  
Marketing pages should not obstruct this journey. Login must remain visible in header and footer.

## 7. Content principles

**Brand naming**  
The official product and platform name is **The Portal Genie**. Use "Portal Genie" only where it reads naturally within body copy. Navigation labels follow the official naming convention.

**Lead with outcomes**  
Communicate what the business and its customers gain before listing capabilities. Every feature should answer: how does this improve the customer's experience?

**Position the category correctly**  
Portal Genie is the Customer Experience Layer for Xero businesses. It connects the power of Xero with the people those businesses serve.

**Avoid weak framing**  
Do not describe Portal Genie as merely a client portal, a document portal, or a Xero integration. It creates a new category.

**Respect Xero**  
Portal Genie extends and enhances Xero. Never imply official Xero endorsement or partnership unless explicitly stated.

**Speak to Xero businesses**  
Audience: business owners, managing directors, finance managers, operations managers, office managers, administrators, accountants, and bookkeepers. Not Xero as a company.

**Keep language clear and premium**  
Conversational plain English. Confident, helpful, professional. No jargon, no salesy hype, no corporate coldness.

**One primary CTA sitewide**  
Book a Demo is the consistent primary action across pages.

**Secondary CTA**  
See How It Works (or context-appropriate variants such as Explore the Platform, View Features, Contact Us) supports evaluation without competing with the primary action.

**Educate before selling**  
Especially on Resources and FAQ sections. Establish authority; reduce uncertainty.

**Use real product proof**  
Prefer screenshots and real workflows over generic illustrations when explaining the platform.

**Brand promise**  
Your customers always know where to go.

**Definition of success**  
A visitor leaves thinking: I already use Xero; Portal Genie is exactly what my business has been missing; it doesn't replace Xero — it completes it.
